# NextCareer — What to Add or Improve Next

## Current state (verified)
The app already has: app-style landing page, Google/PayFast billing, free/premium gating (1 interview/mo, no roadmap, 1 smart apply/mo), Smart Apply with shortlist + salary + match score, AI video interview with face tracking + TTS/STT, interview history, CV builder, cover letter, job match, roadmap, Kanban tracker with reminders, profile, dark/light mode, and Android Play Billing.

## Proposed next improvements
Pick 2–3 and I’ll build them:

1. **ATS Resume Score + Fix Suggestions**
   - Give the CV Builder a real ATS readability score.
   - Show specific fixes: missing keywords, weak verbs, formatting issues, section order.
   - One-click “Rewrite with AI” per section.

2. **Company & Role Research for Interviews**
   - Paste a company name/URL before the interview.
   - AI generates: company summary, likely interview questions, culture fit angle, news/talking points.
   - Premium-only.

3. **Application Analytics Dashboard**
   - Response rate, interviews-to-offers, average days per stage, weekly activity chart.
   - Helps users see what’s working in their job search.

4. **Offline Mode for Tracker**
   - Cache applications locally on native so users can view and update status without signal.
   - Sync back to Supabase when online.

5. **Google Play In-App Review Prompt**
   - Trigger after a positive event (completed interview, 3rd tracker entry, first offer).
   - Increases Play Store rating without leaving the app.

6. **Smart Job Alerts**
   - Save a Smart Apply search and get a push notification when new matching jobs are found.
   - Daily/weekly digest option.

## Recommendation
If you want the biggest user-value lift with the least risk, start with **#1 ATS Score** (makes CV Builder feel complete) and **#5 In-App Review Prompt** (directly helps Play Store ranking).
